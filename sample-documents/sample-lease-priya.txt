RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into this 1st day of October, 2026, by and between Metro Living Properties LLC ("Landlord"), and Priya Sharma ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND OCCUPANCY
Landlord leases to Tenant the premises known as Apartment 4B, 742 Evergreen Terrace, for residential use only. Only the named Tenant may reside in the premises.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL
The term of this Lease commences on November 1, 2026, and ends on October 31, 2027. This Lease shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis at a 15% rent increase unless Tenant provides ninety (90) days advance written notice of intent to vacate prior to the expiration date.

3. RENT AND LATE CHARGES
Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent of $2,200.00, payable in advance on or before the first day of each calendar month. If rent is not received by Landlord by the third (3rd) calendar day of the month, a late fee of $150.00 plus $25.00 for each additional day of delinquency shall immediately become due as additional rent.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $4,400.00 (two months' rent) as a security deposit. Landlord may withhold the deposit for any damages, cleaning fees, or early termination fees, and is not required to place the deposit in an interest-bearing escrow account.

5. EARLY TERMINATION PENALTY
If Tenant terminates or vacates the premises prior to the end of the Lease term, Tenant shall forfeit the full security deposit and shall remain liable for all rent payments due for the remainder of the 12-month term until a replacement tenant is found and executes a lease, plus an administrative re-letting fee of $1,000.00.

6. LANDLORD ACCESS AND INSPECTION
Landlord and Landlord's agents reserve the right to enter the premises at any time without prior notice for inspections, maintenance, repairs, or showing the property to prospective buyers or tenants.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall be solely responsible for all maintenance, minor repairs, and service costs under $300.00, including plumbing clogs, appliance maintenance, and HVAC filter replacements, regardless of pre-existing condition.

8. PETS AND ALTERATIONS
No pets or animals of any kind are permitted on the premises. Tenant shall not paint, install fixtures, or make any structural alterations without Landlord's prior written consent.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State. Any legal disputes arising under this Agreement must be submitted to binding individual arbitration; Tenant explicitly waives the right to a jury trial or class action proceeding.
