RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (NEGOTIATED REVISION)

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into this 1st day of October, 2026, by and between Metro Living Properties LLC ("Landlord"), and Priya Sharma ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND OCCUPANCY
Landlord leases to Tenant the premises known as Apartment 4B, 742 Evergreen Terrace, for residential use only. Only the named Tenant may reside in the premises.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL
The term of this Lease commences on November 1, 2026, and ends on October 31, 2027. This Lease may renew on a month-to-month basis with rent adjustments capped at the local Consumer Price Index (CPI, max 3%), provided either party gives sixty (60) days advance written notice prior to renewal.

3. RENT AND LATE CHARGES
Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent of $2,200.00, payable in advance on or before the first day of each calendar month. A grace period of five (5) calendar days is provided. If rent is not received by the fifth (5th) day, a single flat late fee of $50.00 shall apply.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $2,200.00 (one month's rent) as a security deposit. Landlord shall hold the deposit in a dedicated interest-bearing escrow account and return it within 21 days of lease termination with an itemized statement of any deductions.

5. EARLY TERMINATION
Tenant may terminate this Agreement prior to expiration by providing sixty (60) days advance written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to one (1) month's rent ($2,200.00). Upon payment of said fee and delivery of possession, Tenant shall be released from all further rental obligations.

6. LANDLORD ACCESS AND INSPECTION
Landlord or authorized agents may enter the premises only upon providing at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice, and entry shall occur only during reasonable business hours (9:00 AM - 6:00 PM), except in cases of bona fide emergency threatening life or property.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Landlord shall be responsible for all structural repairs, plumbing, HVAC, electrical systems, and major appliances. Tenant shall maintain the premises in good cleanliness and replace standard light bulbs and HVAC filters supplied by Landlord.

8. PETS AND ALTERATIONS
Tenant may keep one domestic cat upon payment of a refundable $250 pet deposit. Tenant may hang pictures using standard hooks, but shall not make major structural modifications without prior written approval.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement is governed by state residential tenancy law. Disputes shall first be submitted to non-binding mediation; both parties retain their statutory rights to seek relief in local housing/small-claims court.
